About Me

I started coding back in 2017, where I just did simple commands on cmd. I thought it was really cool, until I heard of WSL. WSL, (or Windows Subsystem for Linux), is a way to run a GNU/Linux environment directly on Windows. It allowed me to do so much more with my computer, then one day I wanted to see how an AI could be able to talk to you in a terminal, That's how JTAICB was born. Eventually, I switched to Ubuntu 25.04 itself, that way I can better connect to my coding environment, (also because it's smoother, completely customizable, and simpler to use).
